Simple Process

From Problem to Solution

Making your neighbourhood better takes only a few simple steps. Spot an issue, report it, and follow its journey towards resolution.

01

Spot an Issue

Notice a pothole, overflowing garbage, a broken streetlight, or another civic problem in your area.

02

Report It

Submit the issue with its details, category, photo, and location to help identify the problem.

03

Track Progress

Follow status updates as the report is reviewed and assigned for necessary action.

04

See the Change

Check whether the issue has been resolved and contribute to a better community.

What Makes Spot2Fix Useful

A Smarter Way to Manage Civic Issues

One platform to connect citizen reports with organised tracking, coordinated action, and measurable progress.

Location-Based Reporting

Include issue locations so that civic problems can be identified and addressed more effectively.

Real-Time Status Updates

Follow report statuses from submission and review through ongoing work and resolution.

Priority-Based Management

Organise reported issues by priority to help teams focus on problems needing attention.

Coordinated Assignments

Support the assignment of civic issues to responsible maintenance personnel.

Progress Insights

View report statistics and track the number of submitted, ongoing, and resolved issues.

Citizen Participation

Help citizens take an active role in identifying problems and improving their surroundings.
